Acme Corp Enterprise Policy Handbook

Employee Benefits and Paid Time Off
Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid time off per calendar year. Unused days up to 5 days can be carried forward to the following quarter. All leave requests must be submitted through the enterprise HR portal at least two weeks in advance.

Remote Work and Core Hours
Acme Corp operates on a hybrid working model. Team members are expected to be available during core hours between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM EST. Regular sync meetings occur on Monday mornings.

Information Security and Data Protection
All company laptops must run full-disk encryption and multi-factor authentication (MFA). Sharing customer documentation or source code via unapproved personal cloud drives is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate disciplinary review.
